# Placement assistance instructions

Source: https://app.notion.com/p/3121199ccfe3801d9a3cd3349e179177



This program is designed to support participants in their **interview preparation and job placement journey** after successfully completing the cohort.

The goal is to ensure every serious learner gets structured guidance, validation, and mentorship while applying for jobs.

---

### 📅 Support Duration

After completing the cohort, participants will receive **placement support for 3 months**.

During this period, I will actively help with:

- Interview preparation

- Concept clarification

- Assignment validation

- Job application strategy

- Mock discussions and guidance

- Referrals (based on readiness)


### 🧩 How the Placement Support Works

#### 1️⃣ Assignment Completion & Validation

Participants can complete the **system design / learning assignments** provided during or after the cohort.

Once completed:

- Approach me for review

- We will schedule a discussion call

- I will evaluate your understanding through conversation and reasoning

If you demonstrate **clear conceptual understanding and confidence**, I will consider you ready for referrals.

---

#### 2️⃣ Referral Eligibility

Referrals are **not automatic** — they are based on readiness.

You may be referred if you:

✔ Show strong conceptual clarity

✔ Can explain system design decisions confidently

✔ Demonstrate structured thinking

✔ Show practical understanding, not just theoretical knowledge

The goal is to ensure you are genuinely prepared to perform well in interviews.

---

#### 3️⃣ Independent Job Applications (Recommended)

Participants are strongly encouraged to **apply for jobs on their own** during the support period.

I will assist with:

- Preparation strategy

- Interview guidance

- Concept revision

- Discussion of interview experiences

- Improvement areas

The focus is to maximize opportunities and learning from each interview.

---

### 💬 Communication & Support Channels

You can reach out to me via:

- Discord

- WhatsApp


Please update me when:

- You have an upcoming interview

- You need preparation support

- You want feedback or clarification

- You want assignment validation

---

### 🤝 Mode of Support

Based on urgency, complexity, and availability, support may happen through:

- Quick chat guidance

- Voice call

- Video call

- Concept discussion session

- Interview preparation walkthrough

Support will be provided in the most effective format for the situation.

---

### 🧠 What I Expect From Participants

To make the best use of this program:

✔ Be proactive in learning and applying

✔ Communicate early about interview schedules

✔ Come prepared with questions or topics

✔ Complete assignments sincerely

✔ Focus on understanding, not shortcuts

This is a **mentorship-driven support model**, not a passive service.

---

### 🚀 Final Note

This support is meant for participants who are **serious about improving and putting in consistent effort**.

If you stay committed, actively communicate, and demonstrate strong understanding — I will fully support you in your placement journey.

Let’s work together to make the transition from learning to employment smooth and successful.
