## How job search is a passive process ?

### How to improve visibility on LinkedIn / social media?

1. Update your LinkedIn headline with your core tech stack. Add all possible skills with which you can be searched. Ex: Frontend developer, Reactjs developer, UI developer etc. As recruiters many a time don’t know the Similarity.

1. Get endorsement for you’re skills.

1. Set LinkedIn Job Alerts.

1. Contribute to open source / create some libraries and write articles about it. Ex: Creating a star rating widget.

1. Just spend time on LinkedIn **😊. **More time you spend, chances you appear in search result will increase.


### How to connect with right people on LinkedIn

→ **Start connecting with people from these companies to begin with. **

| **Category** | **Company List** |
| **High-Growth Fintech** | Razorpay, Slice, Paytm, Groww, Zeta |
| **Consulting & MNCs** | Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Accenture |
| **Consumer Product** | Zomato, Blinkit, Swiggy, Urban Company, Zepto |
| **Enterprise Tech** | Oracle, SAP, Cisco, Intuit, Uber |
| **Engineering Services** | Thoughtworks, Persistent Systems, LTIMindtree, Publicis Sapient, EPAM Systems |
| **New-Age Disruptors** | Jupiter, Navi, Upstox, Acko, CoinSwitch |

→** Start following their engineering blogs and add comment wherever you feel relevant. **

→ **Start liking and commenting to their post, wherever it is relevant. Let this be organic. **


### Other strategies

1. Create profiles on many job portals. Ex: instahire, foundit, hirist, shine, monster etc.

1. Attend physical meetups that happen near to you’re place. Go and network with people.

1. If you have some time and interest, try solving a practical problem and make it open source and write a blog about it. Ex: **One sec mobile app. **

1. Start connecting with startup founders. Read startup news from websites like [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/). Connect with new founders on LinkedIn, see what you can do to make them accept you’re request. Ex: Record a video, explain bugs in their product etc.

1. **The "Alumni Loop”: **Check LinkedIn for people who used to work at your current company and are now at the above 30 companies we listed or beyond.
