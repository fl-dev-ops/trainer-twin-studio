---
title: "Assign"
description: "Give learners a published role play to practise."
---

An assignment lets a learner practise a published role play through a personal link (`practiceUrl`), as many times as their credits allow. A learner can only practise the role plays assigned to them. For the whole flow, from assigning to reading the result, see [Role plays end to end](/guides/role-plays).

## Before you assign

You need:

- A learner `userId` from `GET /users`, or just an `email`. Someone new is added to your organization as a learner first (with their starting credits), in the same request.
- A published scenario slug from `GET /scenarios` or the TrainerTwin dashboard.

Scenario and persona configuration remains dashboard-only.

## Create an assignment

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/assignments" \
  --request POST \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY" \
  --header "content-type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "userId": "user_123",
    "scenario": "react-mock-interview"
  }'
```

A new assignment returns `201`:

```json
{
  "assignment": {
    "id": "assignment_123",
    "assignedAt": "2026-09-01T09:30:00.000Z",
    "userId": "user_123",
    "email": "avery@example.com",
    "scenario": "react-mock-interview"
  },
  "practiceUrl": "https://your-org.trainertwin.com/s/AbC123",
  "created": true,
  "emailSent": true
}
```

`practiceUrl` is the learner's personal practice link. Send it through your own channel instead of, or alongside, the TrainerTwin email. It works only for the email it was assigned to: the learner signs in with that email (Google or password).

The email, **New Role Play Assigned**, names the role play and its objective, says how many minutes of practice the learner has, and which email to sign in with.

Creating the same learner-scenario pair again is safe. While the assignment is pending or used, TrainerTwin returns it with `created: false` and does not send another email. A cancelled or expired assignment is reopened instead: it gets a new `practiceUrl`, the learner gets an email, and the response is `201` with `created: true`.

`userId` is `null` when the email belongs to someone who has not joined yet.

<Note>
  Assignment persistence succeeds even when email delivery fails. Check `emailSent` instead of retrying the assignment immediately.
</Note>

## Credits

Someone new to your organization's credits gets **30** (1 credit = 1 minute of practice) from its prepaid balance when first assigned. If the balance can't cover them, the request returns `409` and nothing is assigned. Credits belong to the learner and are shared by every role play and twin, so assigning more role plays doesn't give more credits. A call ends when the learner's credits run out. Give learners more credits in the dashboard.

## Practising again

There's no limit on attempts. Each practice starts a new session from the same `practiceUrl`, and `status` becomes `used` after the first. The link keeps working until you remove the assignment. List an assignment's sessions with `GET /sessions?assignmentId=…`.

## Change an assignment

Move an assignment to another learner, scenario, or both:

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/assignments/assignment_123" \
  --request PATCH \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY" \
  --header "content-type: application/json" \
  --data '{ "scenario": "system-design-interview" }'
```

You can also move it to an `email` address. A changed assignment sends a notification to the target learner and returns a new `practiceUrl` (the old link stops working). Moving it to someone new to your organization's credits gives them their 30 first, or returns `409` when the balance can't cover them. A no-op update returns `changed: false`, the existing `practiceUrl`, and sends no email. Moving onto a learner who already has that scenario assigned returns `409`.

## Inspect an assignment

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/assignments/assignment_123" \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
```

Returns the learner, scenario, current `status`, `practiceUrl`, how often it was practised (`practiceCount`), and when last (`lastPracticedAt`, or `null`). `status` is `pending` (not practised yet), `used` (practised at least once; the link still works), or `cancelled`. Assignments made before links stopped expiring may show `expired`; assigning again reopens them. For an assignment made to the email of someone who has not joined yet, the learner's `id` and `name` are `null`.

List assignments with `GET /assignments`, filtered by `userId`, `email`, or `scenario`.

## Remove an assignment

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/assignments/assignment_123" \
  --request DELETE \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
```

Removing an assignment does not remove the scenario or historical practice sessions.
