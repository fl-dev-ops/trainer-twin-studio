---
title: "Users"
description: "Add, list, update, and remove learners in your organization."
---

The users API manages learners, not trainers. Adding a learner makes them part of your organization right away (TrainerTwin creates their account if they don't have one). They get an email, **You've been added to *your organization* on TrainerTwin**, with a link to your organization's home page, where they sign in with Google or with a code sent to that email. There are no passwords and nothing to accept.

To give someone a role play, you don't need to add them first: [assign](/guides/assignments) it to their email, and they're added the same way, with one email for both.

## Add a learner

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/users" \
  --request POST \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY" \
  --header "content-type: application/json" \
  --data '{ "email": "learner@example.com" }'
```

The response includes the learner and whether the email was delivered (`201`).

```json
{
  "user": {
    "id": "user_123",
    "name": "learner",
    "email": "learner@example.com",
    "emailVerified": false,
    "createdAt": "2026-11-11T09:30:00.000Z",
    "role": "member",
    "memberId": "member_123"
  },
  "created": true,
  "emailSent": true
}
```

<Note>
  A delivery failure does not undo the add. Read `emailSent` and handle follow-up in your platform.
</Note>

Adding someone who is already a learner returns `200` with `created: false` and sends nothing. An email that belongs to one of your trainers (admin or team), or to a learner you removed, returns `409`.

A new learner gets **30 credits** (1 credit = 1 minute of practice) from your organization's prepaid balance. Credits are shared by every twin and role play and are used by the second during calls; learners see them as minutes. When the balance can't cover a new learner, the call returns `409`; contact TrainerTwin to top it up. Give a learner more through `POST /users/{id}/credits` with an Idempotency-Key, or in the dashboard. See [Credits](/guides/credits).

## List learners

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/users?limit=50&offset=0" \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
```

The response contains enabled learners in `users`, newest first, including `creditsGiven` and `creditsLeft`. Add `?email=learner%40example.com` for an exact, case-insensitive lookup. Admin/team and disabled people are not listed. `invitations` is always empty (kept for older integrations).

## Update a learner name

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/users/user_123" \
  --request PATCH \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY" \
  --header "content-type: application/json" \
  --data '{ "name": "Avery Patel" }'
```

Email and organization role changes are not supported through this endpoint. An account used by another organization cannot be renamed here (`account_shared`/409); the person can change their own name in Settings. Sending the current name is a no-op.

## Remove a learner

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/users/user_123" \
  --request DELETE \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
```

A successful removal, including a retry for an already-disabled learner, returns `204 No Content`. Nothing is deleted: the learner is disabled — signed out and unable to sign in, with pending assignments cancelled and open sessions ended. Their account and historical sessions are kept. A removed learner no longer appears in list or detail responses; an organization trainer can re-enable them from Settings → Team and users.
