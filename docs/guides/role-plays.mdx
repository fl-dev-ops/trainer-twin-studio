This guide puts the endpoints together: from a published role play to the learner's result in your platform.

## Before you start

In the [TrainerTwin dashboard](https://dash.trainertwin.com):

1. **Publish the role plays** you want to offer. The API can read them, but not create or edit them.
2. **Create an API key** on the Developer page and keep it on your server.
3. **Check your credits.** Practice time is prepaid: TrainerTwin tops up your organization's credits, and each learner gets credits from that balance. 1 credit = 1 minute of practice. Learners see their credits as minutes.

## Two things a learner needs

| | What it controls | How they get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **An assignment** | *Which* role play they can do, through a personal link, as many times as they like | `POST /assignments` |
| **Credits** | *How long* they can practise, shared by every twin and role play | 30 when first added (by an assignment or `POST /users`); more from the dashboard |

## The flow

```text
Your platform                          TrainerTwin                           Learner
─────────────                          ───────────                           ───────
1. GET  /scenarios      ─────────▶  published role plays
2. POST /assignments    ─────────▶  assigns it; someone new gets 30 credits
   { email, scenario }  ◀─────────  practiceUrl  (+ TrainerTwin email)
3. show "Start practice" ────────────────────────────────────────────▶  opens the link
                                     signs in with the assigned email   ◀──
                                     voice role play, sees minutes left
4. GET  /sessions?userId=…&status=completed
5. GET  /sessions/{id}  ─────────▶  transcript, evidence, recordings
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="List the role plays">
    ```bash
    curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/scenarios" \
      --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
    ```
    Map each `slug` to a course or module in your platform. See [Scenarios](/guides/scenarios).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign one to a learner">
    ```bash
    curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/assignments" \
      --request POST \
      --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY" \
      --header "content-type: application/json" \
      --data '{ "email": "avery@example.com", "scenario": "react-mock-interview" }'
    ```
    An email is enough: someone new is added to your organization as a learner first, in the same request. The response includes `practiceUrl`, the learner's personal link.

    Someone new to your organization's credits gets **30** from its balance. When the balance can't cover them, the request returns `409` and nothing is assigned. Repeating the request is safe: it returns the same assignment and sends no second email. See [Assign](/guides/assignments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the learner to practise">
    Show `practiceUrl` in your platform, for example as a **Start practice** button. TrainerTwin also emails it (see [Emails](#emails-the-learner-gets)).

    The learner opens the link and signs in with **the email the role play was assigned to**, with Google or a code emailed to them. Another account sees a prompt to switch. The role play runs in TrainerTwin as a voice call, not inside your platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    There are no webhooks yet. Check for finished sessions now and then:
    ```bash
    curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/sessions?userId=user_123&scenario=react-mock-interview&status=completed" \
      --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
    ```
    Then read one for its transcript, evidence, and recordings with `GET /sessions/{id}`. Every session has the `assignmentId` it came from. See [Sessions](/guides/sessions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Practising again

There's no limit on attempts: a learner can practise an assigned role play as many times as their credits allow. After a call they can choose **Practise again**, or open the same `practiceUrl` later; each practice starts a new session. A learner whose connection drops mid-call rejoins the same call while it's still open.

The link keeps working until you remove the assignment (`DELETE /assignments/{id}`). To read every practice of one assignment:

```bash
curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/sessions?assignmentId=assignment_123" \
  --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
```

`GET /assignments/{id}` also tells you how often it was practised (`practiceCount`) and when last (`lastPracticedAt`).

## Credits during practice

- Credits are counted by the second while the learner is in a call.
- The learner sees the minutes they have left before they start. A call needs at least **5 minutes** to start.
- When they run out, the call ends with a goodbye from the role play and a "Time's up" message, and the learner can't start another one until they get more.
- A learner has one call at a time in your organization: starting a new one ends any other that is still open.
- Admins' and team members' voice calls use your organization's credits directly (also at least 5 to start).

Giving a learner more credits, choosing how many a new learner starts with, and seeing who is running low are done in the dashboard (**Users** and **Credits**). The API doesn't change credits, except for the 30 a new learner gets.

## Emails the learner gets

| Request | Email | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /assignments` (new or reopened), `PATCH /assignments/{id}` (changed) | **New role play**: the role play, its objective, the credits they have (to use on it as often as they like), and which email to sign in with. Someone new to your organization is told they were added, in the same email. | `practiceUrl`, their personal link |
| `POST /users` | **You've been added to *your organization* on TrainerTwin**, with their credits | Your organization's home page, where they sign in with Google or an email code |

You don't need `POST /users` before assigning a role play: assigning by email adds the learner to your organization first, with one email for both. Every response reports `emailSent`. An assignment or a new learner is kept even when the email fails, so you can send the link yourself.

## Done in the dashboard

- Creating and editing role plays, personas, and voices.
- Adding credits, choosing starting credits, and the Credits page.
- Twins: the API covers role plays only.
