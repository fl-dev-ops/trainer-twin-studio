title: "Practice flow"
description: "Assign a published role play, send the learner to practice, and read the stored result."
---

This is the path an integration usually takes. The field and path names are in [Names and limits](/limits).

## Before the first request

In the [TrainerTwin dashboard](https://dash.trainertwin.com):

1. Publish the role play. This API cannot author one.
2. Create an API key on the Developer API page and store it on your server.
3. Make sure the organization has credits to allocate. `GET /credits` shows the minutes still available. One credit is one minute.

A learner needs both of these:

| They need | What it controls | How they get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An assignment | Which role play they can open, and their personal link | `POST /assignments` |
| Credits | How long they can practice, across every role play | 30 when first added. More from `POST /users/{id}/credits`. |

## Requests

```text
Your server                          TrainerTwin                         Learner
──────────                          ───────────                         ───────
GET  /scenarios                 →    published role plays
POST /assignments               →    assignment + practiceUrl
{ email or userId, scenario }   ←    email to the learner
show practiceUrl                ───────────────────────────────→        opens the link
                                    signs in with the assigned email
GET  /sessions?status=completed
GET  /sessions/{id}             →    transcript, report, recordings
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the role play">
    `GET /scenarios` returns each published role play's `slug`. Store that slug against the course or module in your platform. The assignment field is `scenario`, and the session field is `agentSlug`; both are this slug. See [Role plays](/guides/scenarios).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign it">
    Send the learner's `userId` or `email`, and the slug as `scenario`. Do not send both identifiers.

    A new email is added as a learner in the same request and receives 30 credits once. If the organization balance cannot cover that grant, the response is `409` and no assignment is created.

    Repeating the request for an open assignment returns it and does not send another email. See [Assignments](/guides/assignments).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the learner to practice">
    Put `practiceUrl` behind a button in your platform. TrainerTwin also emails it when `emailSent` is true.

    The learner signs in with the assigned email. Another TrainerTwin account is asked to switch. The call runs in TrainerTwin, not inside your product. A dropped connection rejoins the open call. A later visit starts a new session on the same link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    There is no webhook.

    ```bash
    curl "https://dash.trainertwin.com/api/v1/sessions?userId=user_123&scenario=react-mock-interview&status=completed" \
      --header "x-api-key: $TRAINERTWIN_API_KEY"
    ```

    `GET /sessions/{id}` returns the transcript, stored report, `reportStatus`, evidence, and one-hour recording URLs. A completed call can still have `reportStatus: "generating"`. See [Sessions](/guides/sessions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Credits during the call

- Time is counted by the second.
- A learner needs at least 5 credits to start. They see the minutes left before the call.
- At zero, the call ends. They cannot start another until they receive more credits.
- A learner has one live call at a time in your organization. Starting another ends the open one.
- Trainer previews spend the organization balance and last at most 30 minutes. Learner calls are not under that 30-minute cap.

`GET /users/{id}/credits` is the learner balance. `POST /users/{id}/credits` adds more and requires an `Idempotency-Key`. See [Credits](/guides/credits).

## Emails

| Request | What the learner receives |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /users` | Added to the organization, with their starting credits and a sign-in link |
| `POST /assignments` when a link is created or reopened | The role play, their credit balance, the sign-in email, and `practiceUrl`. A new learner is told they were added in this same email. |
| `PATCH /assignments/{id}` when the destination changes | The same role-play email to the destination learner |
| `POST /users/{id}/credits` when the grant is new | Credits added and the balance. No practice link. |

`emailSent: false` means this request did not get a notification accepted. The learner, assignment, or grant is still saved. A retry of an unchanged assignment or credit grant does not send a second email.

## Not in this API

- Creating or editing role plays, personas, and voices.
- Buying organization credits or changing the plan.
- Starting, ending, or joining the call.
- Twins.
